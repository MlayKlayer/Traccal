# Traccal

**A private, lightweight calorie calculator that works offline.**

Traccal gives you a quick answer when that is all you need, with optional tools for daily tracking, goals, saved foods, nutrition details, and history.

Once you open Traccal online for the first time, the app is saved for offline use in your browser. Your calculations, settings, and logs remain entirely on your device.

## Highlights

- Instant kcal calculations
- Simple, Tracker, and Advanced modes
- Daily goals and food history
- Saved foods and recent calculations
- Light, dark, and automatic themes
- Installable on mobile and desktop
- Offline after the first visit
- No account, cloud storage, or tracking
