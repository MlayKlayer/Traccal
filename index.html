Install & use offline

iOS

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open Traccal in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Turn on Open as Web App, then tap Add.
Android

Android

  1. Open Traccal in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to home screen.
  3. Tap Install and follow the prompt.

For offline use: launch Traccal once with an internet connection and wait a moment. You can then open it from your home screen without internet. Your records stay on this device.
